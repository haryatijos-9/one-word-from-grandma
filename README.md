## Entry

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) scores seven candidate words against the judge brief's five criteria — reads as the single word a grandma would text about AI, sounds like a real grandmother's texting voice, funny in one word, tied to AI/robots/machines, fresh and instantly readable — and writes the winner to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The word

> Robit

The typo a grandma actually makes typing "robot" on a phone keyboard: warm, a little baffled, unmistakably about a machine, and not the obvious straight spelling.
